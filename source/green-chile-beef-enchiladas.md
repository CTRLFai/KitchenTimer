# Green Chile Beef Enchiladas
slug: green-chile-beef-enchiladas
servings: 3 (2 adults, 1 child, plus leftovers)
oven: 375°F
finish: Dinner is on the table. Get leftovers into the fridge within 2 hours.

## Pull these out
### Food
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 medium yellow onion
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 1/2 cups green chile enchilada sauce (2 cans, 10 oz each)
- 3 cups shredded cheese (Monterey Jack, Colby Jack, or a mix)
- 12 corn tortillas
### Gear
- Cast iron skillet
- 9x13 baking dish
- Cutting board and knife
- Cheese grater
- Can opener
- Measuring cups
- Small bowl (spices)
- Spatula
- Paper towels
- Microwave-safe plate
- Hot sauce for the table
- Container for leftovers

## Steps
### Prep: Shred the cheese (6 min)
Start at 4:00 for dinner at about 5:17. The beef should have been thawing in cold water since 3:00. Squeeze the package: it should be soft all the way through.
Shred 3 cups of cheese. Make one pile of 1 cup for the filling and one pile of 2 cups for the top. If it's already shredded, just measure the two piles.

### Prep: Dice the onion (6 min)
Dice **1 medium yellow onion** into small pieces, about the size of a pea.

### Skillet: Heat the skillet (4 min)
Put the cast iron on medium-high with **1 tbsp oil**.
While it heats: open both cans of enchilada sauce (2 cans, 10 oz each, 2½ cups total), put **1 tsp cumin**, **1/2 tsp garlic powder** and **1/2 tsp salt** in the small bowl, and set the 9x13 dish out.
The pan is ready when the oil shimmers and runs easily.

### Skillet: Cook the onion (4 min)
Add the onion. Stir now and then until it's soft and see-through at the edges.

### Skillet: Brown the beef (8 min)
Add **1 lb ground beef**. Break it into small crumbles with the spatula and cook until no pink is left.
In the last minute, sprinkle the spice bowl over it and stir it in. If a lot of grease pools in the pan, tilt it and spoon most of it out.

### Assemble: Finish the filling, sauce the dish (2 min)
Turn off the burner. Stir **1/2 cup green chile enchilada sauce** and **1 cup cheese** into the beef.
Spread **1/2 cup green chile enchilada sauce** over the bottom of the 9x13.

### Oven: Oven on (1 min)
Set the oven to 375°F. It will be hot by the time the dish goes in.

### Assemble: Warm the first tortillas (1 min)
Stack **6 corn tortillas** on the plate, cover with a damp paper towel and microwave 45 seconds. Keep them covered. Cold corn tortillas crack when you roll them.

### Assemble: Roll the first 6 (7 min)
For each tortilla: a heaping ¼ cup of filling in a line across the middle, roll it up snug, and set it in the dish seam side down. Pack them tight, side by side, starting at one end.
If a tortilla cracks, dip the next one in the sauce in the dish for a second before filling it.

### Assemble: Warm the last tortillas (1 min)
Same as before: the other **6 corn tortillas** on the plate, under a damp paper towel, 45 seconds.

### Assemble: Roll the last 6 (7 min)
Fill and roll the same way, packed tight against the first row. If the last one or two won't fit, tuck them along the edge. Spread any leftover filling over the top.

### Assemble: Sauce and cheese on top (2 min)
Pour the remaining **1 1/2 cups green chile enchilada sauce** evenly over everything. Cover the tortilla ends so they don't dry out and go hard.
Spread **2 cups cheese** on top.

### Oven: Into the oven (1 min)
Slide the dish onto the middle rack, uncovered.
Start: oven
Button: It's in the oven

### Oven: Bake (wait)
Nothing to do. Wipe down the counter and set the table. The timer will tell you when to check.
Wait: oven

### Serve: Rest (5 min)
Leave the dish on the stovetop so the sauce thickens a little and the enchiladas hold together when you scoop them.

### Serve: Plate it (2 min)
Two enchiladas for each adult, one for the kid. Put hot sauce on the table for the adults.

## Storage
### Fridge (3 to 4 days)
- Cover the dish or move the leftovers to a container, into the fridge within 2 hours.
- Reheat one portion in the microwave 1½ to 2 minutes, or covered with foil in a 350°F oven for 15 to 20 minutes.
