# Easy American Goulash
slug: american-goulash
servings: 4 (2 adults, 1 child, plus leftovers)
finish: Dinner is on the table. Get leftovers into the fridge within 2 hours.

## Pull these out
### Food
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 yellow onion
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- 1 tbsp jarred minced garlic
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire
- 1 1/2 tsp beef bouillon paste
- 1 1/2 cups hot water (from the kettle)
- 2 cups crushed tomato (from the can)
- 1 can diced tomatoes (15 oz)
- 2 cups elbow macaroni
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 cup shredded cheddar (4 oz)
### Gear
- Dutch oven with its lid
- Wooden spoon
- Cutting board and knife
- Can opener
- Small bowl (spices)
- Kettle
- 2-cup measuring cup
- Heat-safe mug (for the grease)
- Containers for leftovers

## Steps
### Prep: Open and measure (7 min)
Open the crushed tomatoes and the can of diced tomatoes. Measure 2 cups of the crushed tomatoes. Measure the 2 cups of macaroni. In the small bowl, mix the salt, pepper, onion powder, Italian seasoning and paprika. Set the bay leaves, garlic and Worcestershire next to the stove.

### Prep: Dice the onion (6 min)
Dice the onion into small pieces, about the size of a pea.

### Pot: Heat the Dutch oven (4 min)
Set the empty Dutch oven on medium heat. No oil needed; the beef brings its own fat.

### Pot: Brown the beef (10 min)
Add **1 lb ground beef** and **1 yellow onion**. Sprinkle the spice bowl over it: **1/2 tsp salt**, **1/2 tsp black pepper**, **1 tsp onion powder**, **1 tsp Italian seasoning**, **1 tsp sweet paprika**.
Break the beef into small crumbles with the spoon and stir every minute or so. Done when no pink is left and the onion is soft.

### Pot: Kettle on (1 min)
Fill the kettle with at least 2 cups of water and turn it on.

### Pot: Drain the grease (4 min)
Turn the burner off and leave the pot where it is. With a mitt on one handle, tip it slightly so the fat pools on one side, then spoon the fat into the heat-safe mug (not down the drain). Turn the burner back to medium.

### Pot: Make the bouillon (2 min)
In the measuring cup, stir **1 1/2 tsp beef bouillon paste** into **1 1/2 cups hot water** from the kettle until it dissolves.

### Pot: Garlic and Worcestershire (1 min)
Stir **1 tbsp minced garlic** and **1 tbsp Worcestershire** into the beef. Cook 1 minute, until you can smell the garlic.

### Pot: Add everything, bring to a boil (7 min)
Pour in **2 cups crushed tomato**, **1 can diced tomatoes** with their juice, the bouillon from the measuring cup, **2 cups elbow macaroni** and **2 bay leaves**. Stir well, making sure the macaroni is under the liquid.
Turn the heat to medium-high. Move on once it's bubbling across the whole surface.

### Pot: Simmer with the lid on (14 min)
Turn the heat down to medium-low and put the lid on. It should bubble gently, not splatter.
Halfway through (about 7 min), lift the lid and stir, scraping the bottom so the macaroni doesn't stick.
Done when the macaroni is tender and the sauce is thick. It stays saucy, not dry. If the macaroni is still firm, lid back on for 2 to 3 more minutes.

### Serve: Heat off, bay leaves out (1 min)
Turn off the heat. Fish out the 2 bay leaves and throw them away. Taste and add a pinch of salt if it needs it.

### Serve: Fill the bowls (3 min)
Spoon into bowls and top with **1 cup shredded cheddar** between them. The child gets about half an adult bowl.

## Storage
### Fridge (3 to 4 days)
- Into a covered container and the fridge within 2 hours of cooking. Keep the cheese off the leftovers; add it fresh when you reheat.
- The macaroni soaks up sauce overnight. Reheat in the microwave with a splash of water, stirring halfway, until steaming hot all the way through.
