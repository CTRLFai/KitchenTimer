# Smothered Chicken and Air Fryer Green Beans
slug: smothered-chicken
servings: 4 (2 adults, 1 child, plus leftovers)
finish: Dinner is on the table. Leftovers into the fridge within 2 hours.

## Pull these out
### Food
- 1 lb chicken breast, marinated since this morning (leave it in the fridge until its step)
- 4 slices bacon
- 1 yellow onion
- 12 oz sliced mushrooms
- 12 oz fresh green beans
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 cup shredded Colby Jack (4 oz)
### Gear
- Cast iron skillet
- Air fryer
- Sheet of foil big enough to cover the skillet
- Spatula and tongs (tongs for raw chicken only)
- Meat thermometer
- Cutting board and knife
- Large microwave-safe bowl (green beans, then serving)
- Plate with a paper towel (bacon)
- Plate for the onions and mushrooms
- Platter (chicken)
- Containers for leftovers

## Trackers
### fryer: Green beans
- fry1 timed 6 min | Green beans in the air fryer | Shake in
- shake alert | Shake the basket | Pull the basket, shake the beans, slide it back in. | Shaken -> fry2
- fry2 timed 6 min | Green beans, second half | Done check in
- check alert | Check the green beans | Done when bright green with brown spots and just tender when you bite one. | Into the bowl -> done | +2 min -> fry2 (2 min)
- done final | Green beans out | Air fryer off.

## Steps
### Prep: Slice the onion (4 min)
Cut the onion in half from top to bottom, peel it, and slice each half into thin half-moons.

### Skillet: Bacon in, burner on (2 min)
Lay **4 slices bacon** flat in the cold cast iron skillet and set the burner to medium. Starting cold lets the fat melt out slowly.
Turn the vent fan on now. The chicken's marinade has sugar and will smoke a little later.

### Prep: Trim the green beans (6 min)
Snap or cut the stem end off each green bean. Skip this if the bag says trimmed.

### Skillet: Flip the bacon (1 min)
Flip each slice when the bottom is browned. If it isn't yet, give it another minute first.

### Skillet: Bacon out (2 min)
When the bacon is crisp, move it to the paper towel plate. If it's still soft, give it 2 more minutes. Leave the bacon fat in the skillet.

### Skillet: Onions in (2 min)
Add **2 tbsp butter** to the bacon fat and let it melt. Add **1 yellow onion** and sprinkle with **1/4 tsp salt**, **1/4 tsp black pepper** and **1/4 tsp garlic powder**. Stir to coat.

### Skillet: Cook the onions (5 min)
Stir every couple of minutes. They're ready for the mushrooms when soft and starting to turn golden.

### Skillet: Mushrooms in (2 min)
Add **12 oz sliced mushrooms** to the onions and stir them together.

### Skillet: Cook onions and mushrooms (3 min)
Stir every minute or so. The mushrooms let off water first.

### Prep: Season the green beans (3 min)
Stir the mushrooms once first. Microwave **1 tbsp butter** in the large bowl for about 30 seconds, until melted. Add **12 oz fresh green beans**, **1/4 tsp salt** and **1/4 tsp garlic powder** and toss to coat. Leave them in the bowl for now.

### Skillet: Topping out (2 min)
The mushrooms are done when their water has cooked away and they're browned. Scoop the onions and mushrooms onto their plate with the spatula. Keep the burner on medium.

### Skillet: Oil in (1 min)
Add **1 tbsp oil** to the skillet and swirl it around.

### Skillet: Chicken in (2 min)
Take the chicken bag out of the fridge. With the tongs, lift the **1 lb chicken breast** pieces out of the bag and lay them flat in the skillet. Leave the marinade in the bag, seal it, throw it out and wash your hands. From here on, the tongs touch only the chicken.

### Air fryer: Green beans in (1 min)
Spread the green beans in the air fryer basket. Set it to 400°F for 12 minutes and start it. No need to preheat. Tap the button once it's running.
Start: fryer
Button: Air fryer is on

### Skillet: Cook the first side (3 min)
Leave the chicken alone so it browns. If it's smoking hard or going black, turn the burner down a notch.

### Skillet: Flip the chicken (1 min)
Flip each piece with the tongs. The cooked side should be browned with a few dark spots.

### Skillet: Cook the second side (4 min)
Leave it alone again.
If alert: Shake the air fryer basket, then come back to the chicken.

### Skillet: Check the chicken (1 min)
Put the thermometer in the thickest piece. You want 165°F. If it's under, give it 2 more minutes and check again. Wipe the thermometer.
If alert: Do what the green bean alert says, then come back to the chicken.

### Skillet: Topping on (2 min)
Turn the burner to low. With the spatula, pile the onions and mushrooms on the chicken.
If alert: Do what the green bean alert says, then come back to the chicken.

### Skillet: Cheese and foil (2 min)
Cover the chicken with **1 cup shredded Colby Jack** and crumble the bacon over it. Lay the foil over the skillet until the cheese melts.
If alert: Do what the green bean alert says, then come back to the chicken.

### Serve: Plate it (2 min)
Move the chicken to the platter with the spatula. The child gets about half an adult piece.
If alert: Do what the green bean alert says, then come back to plating.
Wait: fryer

### Skillet: Burner off (1 min)
Turn the burner off and leave the skillet on the stove to cool. Vent fan off once the smoke clears.

## Storage
### Fridge (3 to 4 days)
- Into a covered container and the fridge within 2 hours of cooking. Keep the chicken and green beans in separate containers.
- Reheat in the microwave until steaming hot all the way through, or in the skillet over medium-low with foil on top.
