# Smothered Chicken and Griddle Green Beans
slug: smothered-chicken
servings: 4 (2 adults, 1 child, plus leftovers)
finish: Dinner is on the table. Leftovers into the fridge within 2 hours.

## Pull these out
### Food
- 1 lb chicken breast, marinated since this morning (in its bag)
- 4 slices bacon
- 1 yellow onion
- 12 oz sliced mushrooms
- 12 oz fresh green beans
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 cup shredded Colby Jack (4 oz)
### Gear
- Blackstone griddle and its dome
- 2 spatulas (1 for raw chicken, 2 clean) and tongs
- Meat thermometer
- Cutting board and knife
- Sheet pan or tray to carry everything out
- Plate with a paper towel (bacon)
- Serving bowl (green beans) and a platter (chicken)
- A bowl to hold the chicken bag on the tray
- Containers for leftovers

## Steps
### Prep: Slice the onion (4 min)
Cut the onion in half from top to bottom, peel it, and slice each half into thin half-moons.

### Griddle: Griddle on (2 min)
Light the Blackstone and set every burner to medium. It needs about 10 minutes to heat, which is the next two steps.

### Prep: Trim the green beans (6 min)
Snap or cut the stem end off each green bean. Skip this if the bag says trimmed.

### Prep: Load the tray and carry it out (4 min)
On the tray: bacon, onion, mushrooms, green beans, butter, oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, cheese, tongs, 2 spatulas, thermometer, the paper towel plate, the serving bowl, the platter and the dome. Put the chicken bag in a bowl of its own at one end, away from the vegetables. Carry it all out.

### Griddle: Bacon on, left side (1 min)
Lay **4 slices bacon** flat on the left side of the griddle.

### Griddle: Onions on, right side (2 min)
Melt **2 tbsp butter** on the right side. Add **1 yellow onion** and sprinkle with **1/4 tsp salt**, **1/4 tsp black pepper** and **1/4 tsp garlic powder**. Toss to coat.

### Griddle: Cook bacon and onions (7 min)
Flip the bacon when the bottom is browned. Stir the onions every couple of minutes.
When the bacon is crisp, move it to the paper towel plate. Leave the bacon fat on the left side.

### Griddle: Mushrooms in (2 min)
Add **12 oz sliced mushrooms** to the onions and stir them together.

### Griddle: Green beans on, middle (2 min)
Melt **1 tbsp butter** in the middle. Add **12 oz fresh green beans**, **1/4 tsp salt** and **1/4 tsp garlic powder**. Toss to coat.

### Griddle: Oil the left side (1 min)
Spread **1 tbsp oil** into the bacon fat on the left side.

### Griddle: Chicken on, left side (2 min)
With the tongs, lift the **1 lb chicken breast** pieces out of the bag and lay them on the left side. Leave the marinade in the bag, seal it and set it aside for the trash. From here on, the tongs touch only raw chicken.

### Griddle: Right side to low (1 min)
Turn the right burner to low so the onions and mushrooms just stay warm. Stir them and toss the green beans.

### Griddle: Cook the first side (3 min)
Leave the chicken alone so it browns. Toss the green beans every minute. If the chicken is browning too fast (the marinade has sugar), turn the left burner down a notch.

### Griddle: Flip the chicken (1 min)
Flip each piece with spatula 1. The cooked side should be browned with a few dark spots.

### Griddle: Cook the second side (4 min)
Keep tossing the green beans. They're done when bright green with some brown spots and just tender when you bite one.

### Griddle: Check the chicken (1 min)
Put the thermometer in the thickest piece. You want 165°F. If it's under, give it 2 more minutes and check again. Wipe the thermometer.

### Griddle: Green beans into the bowl (1 min)
Scoop the green beans into the serving bowl with spatula 2.

### Griddle: Smother and melt (3 min)
With spatula 2, pile the onions and mushrooms on the chicken. Cover with **1 cup shredded Colby Jack** and crumble the bacon over it. Put the dome over the chicken until the cheese melts.

### Serve: Chicken to the platter (1 min)
Move the chicken to the platter with spatula 2. The child gets about half an adult piece.

### Griddle: Burners off (1 min)
Turn every burner off. Scrape the griddle after dinner while it's still warm.

## Storage
### Fridge (3 to 4 days)
- Into a covered container and the fridge within 2 hours of cooking. Keep the chicken and green beans in separate containers.
- Reheat in the microwave until steaming hot all the way through, or on a skillet over medium-low with the lid on.
