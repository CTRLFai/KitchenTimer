# Smothered Chicken: Marinate (do this first)
slug: smothered-chicken-marinade
servings: 4 (2 adults, 1 child, plus leftovers)
finish: Into the fridge it goes. At least 2 hours, up to overnight. Open "Smothered Chicken and Griddle Green Beans" about an hour before dinner.

## Pull these out
### Food
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp onion and garlic powder, half of each
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp jarred minced garlic
- 1/2 cup Texas Roadhouse Classic Steak Sauce
### Gear
- Cutting board and knife
- Gallon zip bag or a covered bowl

## Steps
### Prep: Cut the chicken thinner (3 min)
Lay each piece of **1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast** flat, hold your hand on top, and slice it in half the long way so each piece is about 1/2 inch thick. Wash your hands and the board.

### Prep: Season and sauce (3 min)
Put the chicken in the bag. Add **1/2 tsp black pepper**, **1 tsp onion and garlic powder** (1/2 tsp of each), **1/2 tsp smoked paprika**, **1 tbsp minced garlic** and **1/2 cup Texas Roadhouse Classic Steak Sauce**. Seal and squish until every piece is coated, then put it in the fridge.

## Storage
### Fridge (up to 1 day)
- Raw chicken in marinade keeps in the fridge until tomorrow night at the latest.
