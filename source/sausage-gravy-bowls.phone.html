Storage and reheating

Fridge (4 servings)

  • Eat within 4 days.
  • Bowl: lid cracked, microwave 1½ to 2 min, stirring halfway. Or air fry the potatoes at 375°F for 5 min for crisp edges.
  • Gravy: it sets up thick in the fridge. Add a splash of milk, microwave 45 to 60 sec, stir, pour over.

Freezer (4 servings)

  • Best within 2 months.
  • Thaw: move a bowl bag and a gravy bag to the fridge the night before.
  • Bowl: empty into a microwave-safe bowl, cover loosely, microwave 2 to 2½ min, stirring halfway.
  • Gravy: it may look grainy or separated after freezing. Add a splash of milk, microwave 1 min, and stir hard. It comes back smooth.
