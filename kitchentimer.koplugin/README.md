# Kitchen Timer

A KOReader plugin that walks you through a recipe step by step, at the moment each step is needed.

Open it from **Tools > Kitchen Timer**, or bind **Open Kitchen Timer** to a gesture.

- Pick a recipe, check the **Pull these out** list, press **Start**.
- Each step shows what to do, with its timer and any oven timers underneath the title.
- When something is ready the whole screen turns into an alert card and the frontlight flashes. Tap the button on the card.
- The Kindle stays awake while you cook. It is released when you finish, leave, or the screen closes.
- Long waits at the end of a recipe (a fridge chill) are not timed here: the last step tells you to set a timer on your phone or stove.

Updates arrive over Wi-Fi once a day (never during a cook), or from **Tools > Kitchen Timer > Check for updates**.
Recipes are part of the update: new recipes show up after one.

Settings files are all named `kitchentimer_*.lua`; Relic is not touched.
